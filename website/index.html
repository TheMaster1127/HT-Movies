📖 HT-Movies Master Documentation

1. Profile Saving & Auto-State (F5 Safe)

The engine now explicitly saves every single move you make to your active Profile. Every slider dragged, every checkbox ticked, and every search query typed is instantly cached.

If you press F5, the engine will flawlessly boot up exactly where you left off. Switching between Profiles acts as a hard boundary—changing to a "New Profile" will wipe the interface clean without affecting your main save.

2. Multi-Word "Pipe" Searching

The Search Bar is highly advanced. You can use the Pipe character ( | ) to enforce that multiple distinct words must exist in the same movie's Title, Summary, or Cast.

Example: Typing Pitt | action forces the engine to find records containing BOTH strings.
Space Sensitivity: The search respects exact spacing! Searching for tree (with a space before it) guarantees you find words that start with "tree", strictly excluding matches like "free" or "three".

3. Tri-State Toggle Filters

The core of the engine. Clicking any Genre or AI Trait cycles through three distinct modes:
⬛ Grey (Neutral): The engine ignores this trait completely.
✅ Green (Must Have): Strict inclusion. The database must confirm this trait is true.
❌ Red (Banned): Strict exclusion. The database must confirm this trait is false. No exceptions.

4. Advanced Show Filters (The Grace Season)

When filtering Episodes per Season, the engine automatically allows exactly one season to violate your rule if the show has 2 or more seasons (this allows for shortened finales or pilots). If a show only has 1 season, it strictly obeys your min/max limits.

5. Advanced Cast Engine

Clicking an actor cycles them through your preferences:
Green = Preferred Cast. Red = Banned Cast.

Cast Sorting vs Filtering: By default, picking Preferred actors just lets you Sort them to the top of the grid. But, using the dropdown in the sidebar, you can mandate that a movie includes ANY or ALL of your preferred actors.

6. AI Data & Quirks

Clicking the `Full Info` button reveals exactly what parameters the AI assigned to the movie. The table is explicitly split into Standard DB Info and AI Intel.

Note: The AI operates on best-effort parsing from Wikipedia. You may occasionally find funny artifacts (like Animals classified as having high IQs, or Nature Documentaries having "Action Intensity"). This is part of the fun of raw AI data parsing!

7. The Graveyard & Actions

Hide: Erases the movie from your grid entirely. You will never see it again unless you toggle "Show hidden" in Settings.
Reject: Pushes the movie to the bottom "Rejected" graveyard pile.
Watch Later: Prompts you for a Priority Level (1-10) so you can sort your backlog.

8. The Clear All & Settings

Clear All: The massive ♻️ Clear All button instantly wipes out every slider, toggle, text box, and cast selection, auto-refreshing you to a perfectly clean slate.
Settings: Change Hex Colors of the dark mode layout, and drag/drop Sidebar tabs to re-order them exactly how you like.
